### ***✨ Features***
🎮 Three difficulty levels — Easy (3×3), Normal (4×4), and Hard (5×5).
🎨 Five color themes — Default, Solar, Ocean, Forest, Urban, and Cyberpunk.
⏱️ Game statistics — Track your moves, elapsed time, and personal bests.
📱 Responsive controls — Play with a mouse, keyboard, or touchscreen.
🏆 Win celebrations — Animated victory modal and confetti.
📲 Progressive Web App — Installable on supported devices.
🔌 Offline support — Keep playing with a service worker.

### ***🚀 Getting Started***
Clone or download this repository.
Open index.html in your browser to play.
For PWA installation and offline functionality, serve the project through localhost or HTTPS.

### ***🕹️How to Play***
Select your preferred difficulty and theme.
Click or slide a tile next to the empty space to move it.
Arrange the numbered tiles in ascending order.
Complete the puzzle in as few moves and as little time as possible!
